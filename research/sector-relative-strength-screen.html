    Why these defaults: the lookback study

    Mean forward excess return over Nifty 500 after each event, minus the average sector's excess on any day. Events from 19 sector indices with at least 5 years of history (2011–2026), entry at the next day's close, one event per sector per 63 sessions. Small number = t-stat, clustered by month. The outlined cell is the combination selected above.

    Both legs are needed. RS highs alone or price highs alone add about 1% in 6 months, which is noise. Together, RS at a 1-year high plus a 2–5 year price high adds 3.0–3.4% (t 2.1–2.6), and the neighbouring cells also sit around 2–2.5%. That's a plateau, not a single lucky cell.

    Modest and lumpy. About 125 events in 15 years. Hit rate is about 60% at 6 months but only 50% at 12 months, so the mean is carried by a minority of big winners. 2023 was strong and 2024 was negative. This is an event study on price indices, not a costed portfolio backtest.

    Overlapping sectors. Bank, Private Bank and Fin Services, or PSE and CPSE, often signal together. Treat them as one bet, not three.
