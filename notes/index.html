Notes from the desk

Short and dated. Mostly things my own backtests told me that I did not want to hear. Each one links to the full write-up.

  • 19 Nov 2026

    Natural gas: great backtest, failed holdout, binned

    The hedged expiry-day straddle that keeps part of the premium on Nifty loses money on natural gas, and a gas selling book that passed training and validation failed its holdout.

  • 11 Nov 2026

    Slippage eats first: one point a fill and most of the edge is gone

    In a backtest of my Nifty expiry-day book, half a point of slippage per fill removed about a third of the no-slippage result, and one point removed nearly three-quarters.

  • 10 Nov 2026

    Crude on expiry evening: the premium is real, and unhedged you still give it back

    Across 46 MCX crude-oil expiries, realised volatility in the evening session ran below implied on 89% of days, yet only a delta-hedged short straddle kept a statistically significant share of the premium (backtest, Jan 2023 to Oct 2026, net of assumed slippage).
